**Subject**: Request for Aggregate Administrative Data for Academic Graduation Research: *Spatial Estimation of Economic Activity in Amman*

Dear Respected Directors and Municipal Colleagues,

Peace and blessings upon you.

The Department of Business Analytics at the German Jordanian University (GJU) extends its highest esteem and gratitude to the Greater Amman Municipality for its steadfast public service and dedication to the sustainable urban development of our capital city.

We write to respectfully request your academic cooperation and support for an undergraduate graduation thesis project in Business Analytics & Business Intelligence. The study, entitled **"A Business Intelligence Framework for Estimating Neighborhood-Level Shadow Economic Activity in Amman Using Open Geospatial Data,"** aims to design a geospatial analytical tool that supports municipal urban planning, infrastructure allocation, and micro-business formalization.

### Data Elements Requested:
To establish an empirical formal baseline across Amman, we respectfully request aggregate, district-level data for the **22 Administrative Districts of GAM** (e.g., Al-Madinah, Zahran, Al-Abdali, Basman, Marka, Sweileh, Wadi Al-Seer, etc.):
1. **Total Number of Formally Issued & Active Commercial/Vocational Licenses** (*Rukhas Mihaniyah*) aggregated per administrative district for the years **2023 or 2024**.
2. **Broad Sectoral Breakdown** (if readily available at the district level, e.g., retail, trade, crafts/industrial, food services).

### Academic & Ethical Safeguards:
- **Strictly Aggregate & Non-Confidential**: We do **not** seek individual business names, taxpayer identification numbers, commercial registration codes, or proprietary financial records. Only macro-level counts per district are needed.
- **Academic Research Only**: The data will be used solely for non-commercial graduation research and will not be shared with external commercial entities.
- **Constructive, Non-Punitive Purpose**: The objective is to identify spatial infrastructure gaps (such as designated public markets, formal kiosks, and pedestrian amenities) and support formalization incentives for micro-enterprises, rather than punitive code enforcement.

A copy of the complete study findings, interactive dashboard, and spatial intelligence insights will be gladly presented to the Greater Amman Municipality upon graduation defense.

We thank you immensely for your time, consideration, and continuous support of Jordan's academic and analytical youth.
